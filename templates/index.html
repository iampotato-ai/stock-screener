                        ❓
                    

                    

                        

                            Understanding Episodic Pivots (EPs)
                        

                        

                            Comprehensive guide to the criteria used for quantitative and qualitative scoring.
                        

                    

                

                
            

            

                
                
                

                    

                        💡 What is an Episodic Pivot?
                    

                    

                        An Episodic Pivot (EP) is a powerful quantitative setup popularized by legendary traders. It marks a major structural trend reversal or acceleration where a stock breaks out on exceptional volume (RVOL) and price momentum, triggered by a highly material catalyst (such as stellar earnings, multi-million dollar order wins, rating upgrades, or strategic mergers) that forces the institutional market to suddenly re-evaluate and reprice the equity.
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        THE 5 PILLARS OF EP SCORING
                    

                    

                        
                        
                        

                            

                                📊
                            

                            

                                Relative Volume (RVOL)
                                
                                    Represents institutional footprint. A true EP requires RVOL ≥ 2.0x (and often > 5.0x to 10.0x), indicating aggressive institutional accumulation that cannot be hidden.
                                
                            

                        


                        
                        

                            

                                ✨
                            

                            

                                Catalyst Score (Intensity)
                                
                                    Measures the significance of corporate developments. High-intensity catalysts include positive earnings surprises (YoY growth acceleration), major contract/order wins, regulatory approvals, rating upgrades, or joint ventures.
                                
                            

                        


                        
                        

                            

                                📈
                            

                            

                                Close Location Percentage (Conviction)
                                
                                    Where the price closes within the daily high-low range. A high close location (≥ 50%, ideally > 75%) indicates persistent buy conviction that absorbed all intraday supply.
                                
                            

                        


                        
                        

                            

                                👁️‍🗨️
                            

                            

                                Neglect Score (Institutional Space)
                                
                                    Higher scores indicate low institutional ownership, light analyst coverage, or high retail concentration. Highly neglected stocks reprice much more violently when a powerful catalyst emerges.
                                
                            

                        


                        
                        

                            

                                ⚡
                            

                            

                                Repricing Score (Potential Momentum)
                                
                                    Estimates the headroom for momentum continuation. Evaluates historical price speed and volatility to identify candidates primed for persistent multi-week trends.
                                
                            

                        


                    

                


                
                

                    ℹ️
                    EP Scores are computed using a proprietary weighted ensemble of the 5 Pillars combined with real-time news indexing.
