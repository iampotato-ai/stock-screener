# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Active swing and intraday traders in the Indian equity market (NSE) who seek institutional‑grade screening, AI‑powered forecasting, pattern recognition, and real‑time alerts to identify high‑probability trading opportunities.

## Product Purpose
Provide an institutional‑grade trading dashboard that aggregates live data (price, announcements, bulk/deals, corporate events, news), applies advanced deep‑learning predictions, and surfaces actionable scores (swing, intraday momentum, EP, Bollinger‑Band, etc.) to help users make confident, timely trading decisions.

## Brand Personality
3‑word personality: **Flagship, Accessible, Professional**
Reference sites/apps: TradingView (charting & community), Bloomberg Terminal (depth & reliability), Zerodha Kite (clean Indian‑market UI).
Anti‑references: Overly complex terminal‑only platforms, superficial retail apps with minimal data, cluttered dashboards that obscure actionable insights.

## Design Principles
1. Institutional‑grade analytical rigor – prioritize data quality, transparent calculations, and confidence scoring.
2. Actionable insights with minimal latency – surface the most relevant signals first; keep interactions fast and responsive.
3. Progressive disclosure – expose core metrics by default, hide advanced analytics behind expandable sections to reduce cognitive load.
4. Consistent, accessible interaction patterns – uniform button states, keyboard navigation, focus‑visible indicators, and ARIA labels throughout.
5. Data‑driven confidence scoring – use ensemble forecasting, model weighting, and clear visual cues (e.g., conviction bars) to convey prediction reliability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: keyboard‑navigable, sufficient color contrast (≥4.5:1 for text), focus‑visible outlines, ARIA labels for icon‑only controls, and respect for prefers‑reduced‑motion.
