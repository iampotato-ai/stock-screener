# Brag Plan: MomentumScan

## What is this app?
MomentumScan is an institutional-grade swing & intraday momentum stock screener for the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), combining Episodic Pivot scoring, Market Regime gauges, and EnsembleCast AI multi-model predictions.

## The angle
Trading NSE stocks without multi-model AI signals and Episodic Pivot tracking is like flying blind. MomentumScan aggregates Kronos (deep learning), Meta Prophet, and ARIMA forecasts alongside institutional accumulation alerts (Bull Snort scanner) to give Indian equity traders an unfair quantitative edge.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Stop guessing NSE breakouts." Dark glassmorphic cockpit interface with an animated Market Regime Speedometer dialing from 0 to 84 (Extreme Greed) with an emerald aura glow.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Institutional Accumulation & EP Scanner**: Color-coded volume bars (Blue Accumulation, Orange Dry-Up) popping up for top momentum stocks (TATAMOTORS, RELIANCE, INFOSYS) with Episodic Pivot trigger badges.
- **EnsembleCast AI Multi-Model Forecast**: Multi-segment model weight bar (Kronos 45% Purple, Prophet 35% Orange, ARIMA 20% Cyan) animating into place, locking in a glowing HIGH CONVICTION badge.

## Outro / punchline
"Your Institutional Cockpit for NSE." Clean logo reveal with `MomentumScan` typography, emerald accent outline, and shareable CTA.

## User flow worth showing
Scan Nifty stocks → Filter Episodic Pivots & Bull Snort Accumulation → Trigger EnsembleCast Multi-Model AI Deep Dive → Lock in HIGH CONVICTION trade signal.
